# Demos

Steel is the reference compiler. `target/release/steel --run demo.ano` executes a demo. BQN files may explain an array transformation but are not semantic or differential oracles.

The numbers in the quarantine block of `todo/TODO.md` are decommissioned. Their files remain rewrite inputs and do not count as evidence.
