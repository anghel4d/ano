# noita

Noita's wand as a witness suite: the spell cards of a build-a-projectile game translated card by card, then composed rung by rung into a firing wand. The mapping: a projectile spell is a spawn (an append — keys mint, parent records the caster); a stat modifier is a masked value write over the flight group; multicast is barrier batching; a formation is the replicate's per-copy index; a behavior modifier is a marker component plus a target relationship plus a standing rule; the trigger card is a rule that turns a hit into a cast at the impact; the material world is the 10-conways lattice under a fuel guard. Wand slot order is program order — the barrier makes each card read the previous card's commit. The nN names are Noita witnesses, not ano-examples.md rows.

- `n1-cast.bqn` — Spark Bolt, Double Spell, Formation - Trifurcated: the cast appends, the mana drain shares the cast's barrier, the fan is polar off the caster with index bound per copy; minting two keys in one barrier equals minting one then one — the commutation that licenses the twin's split Double Spell.
- `n2-modifiers.bqn` — Damage Plus and Heavy Shot: [DP, HS] gives 26, [HS, DP] gives 16 from the same two cards; two Damage Plus in one barrier merge additively to 23, and last-wins commit (13) is the pinned counterexample; the bomb bystander keeps its stats.
- `n3-homing.bqn` — the homing card split into data and law: +Homing and target are columns the cast writes, the steer is an installed rule whose body is one masked gather-subtract; one tick halves the gap, the unmarked bolt flies straight.
- `n4-trigger.bqn` — collision as the theta-join comprehension marking both sides, plain bolts break, and the trigger rule spawns the payload at the carrier's own pos while consuming the carrier in one barrier; the payload's parent names a row that barrier removed — the identity boundary pinned as data.
- `n5-alchemy.bqn` — the detonation, spelled twice with one meaning: the raw coordinate-disc predicate over the x/y fields (column arithmetic, nothing else) and the anchored frame `Oil @ blast(5) at Firebolt.pos`, the origin a mirror-read of the payload's chunk position — same cells, idempotent pair, the equivalence pinned by the post-state. Spread is the conways stencil under a fuel guard, one ring per tick down the vein; the pond never enters a mask, so water cannot burn. The `-b` twin adds consumption (`Fire & Oil => -Oil`) in the SAME tick: spread rings the oil that consume is removing, both reading one pre-state — §11's shared barrier, the synchrony a per-rule commit breaks in either order. The `-c` twin lets the clock beat twice: spread installed alone rings once, a query counts the burning cells, and consume's install brings the second beat — spread, still installed, rings again while consume burns under the same pre-state; installs persist across ticks, and the late joiner costs the first ring nothing.
- `n6-wand.bqn` — the assembled wand [Twin Spell, Damage Plus, Trigger, Firebolt] end to end: cast, cards, flight, the join, a scoped-fold damage spend, detonation and one performed ring in the chunk, and the trigger rule minting payloads at both impacts — installed last, so the file's one clock beat lands after the whole timeline. The detonation anchors at `impact`, a bound point — the deictic form of the frame anchor, the host having resolved the payload's world pos into chunk coordinates.

The w-series is the wand-construction stress test: strip the clock, keep the evaluator, and Noita's cast-block algorithm itself becomes column calculus. The finding, recorded in ISSUES.md: no new operator, no new statement form, no grammar change — the draw is a scan, the wrap is index arithmetic, binding is an order join, reflection is a table read.

- `w1-cast-block.bqn` — the draw is a scan: the block's running balance is `1 + +\ (drawAdd - 1)` along slot order and membership is the positive prefix-MINIMUM (negated max-scan, prev over the deck view); the -b twin is the recovery trap — the balance climbs back to 1 after the block already died, so bal >= 0 is the wrong evaluator, pinned as such.
- `w2-wrap.bqn` — the deck is a ring: the cast pointer turns the slot line into a rotation by modular index arithmetic (Tier 1 — the slot ground is regenerable, nothing moves); the balance runs along the rotation via scan-along, whose write-back conjugates through the order's grade; the modifier hung at the buffer's end lands on the wand's first slot — the classic wrap trick, pinned.
- `w3-modifier-chain.bqn` — modifier binding is a reverse min-scan: "next projectile at or after me", multicast markers passed through, so cards chain through a Triple onto its first bolt; the runnable twin uses the collapsed case (all mods before all bolts: one scoped fold), and the .bqn pins the interleaved wand where the shortcut dies and the general join is the evaluator. The `-b` and `-c` twins run the general join on that interleaved wand, both legal spellings: `-b` takes the relation as data (an order-derived srel, fibers = the .bqn's NextP inverted, consumed by the ex36 γ), `-c` computes it as value (`scan(min) … along revDeck` fills the bind column, and the key-valued column itself stands in relation position — `Bind'`, the value-level rel, inverse fibers into the same γ).
- `w4-greek.bqn` — Alpha, Gamma, Omega, Mu as one-line selections over the wand's own table: reflection is free when the program is data — a grade-and-take, a replicate, a mask — and parent pins which slot each letter copied. No quotation, no staging.
- `w5-ledger.bqn` — the timeless resource model: mana, cast delay, recharge as columns, a block's cost as scoped folds broadcast into the wand row; Add Mana credits in the barrier the block debits (the §10 additive merge, order-free); the Chainsaw's negative recharge cancels the bolts' exactly — the rapid-fire trick as a ledger fact, no clock anywhere.
- `w6-eval.bqn` — quotation: `eval "SparkBolt , Damage += 10"` equals the statement written directly. The literal is spliced at parse time — reflection priced as program text, the footprint still visible to §11's static check; dynamic strings are interpreter territory and stay out.

Everything the suite surfaced is now fixed. The emitter conformance bugs (none of them grammar): the same-column `;` batch that committed last-wins where §10 demands the merge (n2-modifiers-c now runs the additive pair; `+=` beside `*=` is rejected with "no merge law"); the single spawn slot that made one-barrier Double Spell impossible (n1 now mints both prototypes in one batch); the scan-along write-back that skipped the Tier-2 conjugation; scan(min) emitting max; the mixed-world despawn crash against lattice srels; the one-shot rule tick that forgot its installs (rules now persist across the harness's clock beats — n5-c fires spread twice). The two spec-side items landed next round: the shared rule barrier (n5-b runs spread+consume in one tick; c3-b runs bloom+wither under the guard-complement certificate) and the frame join (the anchored frame: `@ frame(args) at origin`, the mirror-read form in n5, the deictic-binding form in n6). What remains open is listed in ISSUES.md. The cast queue's clock (delay, recharge, regeneration as time) stays with ano-time.md and the host; w5 models its data.
