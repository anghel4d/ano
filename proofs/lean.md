# Lean semantic kernel

Status: three semantic-kernel milestones machine-checked with Lean 4.30.0: the first ten-result kernel, the bounded spatial registry-and-command kernel, and the affine/interpolation/live-position contract. The kernel uses Lean `Std` only and lives under `proofs/Ano/`.

Run `cd proofs && lake build` from the repository root. The umbrella target imports every proof and negative compile-time witness. The umbrella build has 23 jobs; the repository-wide Nix check is the final integration command.

## First milestone map

1. Layout round-trip: `Layout.decode_encode` and `Layout.encode_decode` prove that a dense buffer and its semantic field encode and decode without loss.
2. Layout-permutation invariance: `Layout.change_encode`, `Layout.decode_change`, `Layout.change_refl`, and `Layout.change_trans` prove that relayout changes storage order but not the decoded field.
3. Reindex identity: `Field.reindex_id`.
4. Reindex composition: `Field.reindex_comp` and `Field.reindex_comp_operator`.
5. Selection gather/scatter correctness: `Selection.gather_scatter`, `Selection.gather_modify_scatter_selected`, `Selection.gather_modify_scatter_unselected`, and `Selection.gather_modify_scatter_exact` use the frozen selection inclusion for both directions.
6. Injective-scatter determinism: `Scatter.injective_scatter_deterministic`, `Scatter.at_most_one_writer`, `Effects.assignment_fiber_subsingleton`, and `Effects.assignment_occurrence_fiber_subsingleton`; `Effects.executeCertifiedAssignment` requires a complete duplicate-free schedule and an injective destination map.
7. Commutative-merge permutation independence: `Effects.commutative_merge_permutation_independent` and `Effects.executeMerge_permutation_independent` require an explicit `CommMonoidLaw` witness.
8. Spawn cardinality and source lineage: `Copies.length_enumerate`, `Copies.enumerate_nodup`, `Copies.mem_enumerate`, `Copies.source_unique`, and the `Copies.gather_*` laws; `Allocation` proves old/fresh disjointness and injective allocation of copy slots.
9. Fixed-field preservation under spawn: `World.spawn_fixed`, `World.spawn_field`, and `World.spawn_wellFormed` leave every registered fixed field definitionally unchanged while advancing the entity population under the schema's spawn-preservation law.
10. World well-formedness through arbitrary finite successful ticks: `perform_ok_wellFormed`, `ticks_wellFormed`, and `Effects.validated_ticks_wellFormed` preserve local field invariants and the schema-wide invariant over population plus all registered fields.

Checked assignment validation is also executable. `Effects.validateRows` traverses the complete duplicate-free query schedule, rejects missing destinations and repeated destination values, and returns evidence from which `CheckedAssignment.destination_injective` follows. `Effects.invalid_destination_atomic`, `Effects.collision_atomic`, and `Effects.validation_rejection_atomic` prove that every such validation failure returns the exact input world.

## Second spatial milestone map

Nominal boxes: `Boxed.coordinate_site` and `Boxed.site_coordinate` prove the round trips between a shape-indexed `Box` and one nominal site domain. `SpatialRegistry.readBoxedField_boxed`, `readBoxedField_spawn`, and `readBoxedField_update_boxed` keep the registry's box witness attached to a fixed field through spawn and ordinary updates.
2. Nominal frames and explicit maps: `FrameSchema`, `Point`, and `SpatialVector` keep equal raw carriers in different frames foreign. `PointMap.refl_trans`, `PointMap.trans_refl`, and `PointMap.trans_assoc` prove identity and composition for explicitly supplied point maps; `Placement.map_refl` and `Placement.map_trans` prove the corresponding placement laws. These are typed function laws, not affine torsor or linear-algebra laws.
3. Registry authority: `SpatialRegistry` grants separate tokens for position views, boxes, semantic lineage, frame maps, offset embeddings, and situated habitats. `SpatialRegistry.readPosition_apply` and `reframe_apply` expose only the carrier and frame named by their token, while `LocatedRows.destination_accepted` and `locate_placed` retain the registered locator evidence.
4. Sealed lineage: `Lineage` has a private constructor. Its public constructors cover identity, composition, selection, product projections, replication source, registered cross-habitat maps, and successful registered localization. `Lineage.gather_refl`, `gather_comp`, and `gather_comp_operator` prove the pullback laws without admitting a physical layout as semantic lineage.
5. Typed localization and sampling: `Locator.locate_eq_some_iff` and `locate_eq_none_iff` characterize successful and missing lookup against an independent functional relation. `Situated.placement_injective` and `accepts_placement` follow from the placement-locator round trip. `Locator.sample?_*`, `LocatorRows.sample_agrees_with_partial`, and registered `LocatedRows.sample_agrees_with_locator` prove that successful point-to-cell lookup gathers the declared cell.
6. Typed scatter: `UniqueLocatorRows.scatter_hit`, `scatter_miss`, and `scatter_deterministic` require injective located destinations. `UniqueRegisteredRows.scatter_hit` and `scatter_miss` carry the same hit/miss law through a registry `SituatedToken`; this constructs a field value and is not yet a world-level component write.
7. Deterministic support choice: `SupportSpec.Best` combines the declared candidate predicate, total score order, stable surface key, and tie-uniqueness law. `SupportSpec.best_functional`, `SupportProjector.locate_eq_some_iff_best`, `locate_eq_none_iff_no_best`, and `best_functional` prove that a successful projection is the unique declared best hit and that a miss means no best hit exists.

Supported batches: `ResolvedSupportBatch.hit_best` certifies one declared best hit per frozen seed, while `ResolvedSupportBatch.position_supported` proves support after pairing the batch with an explicit `RestingPose`. `PreparedBatch` retains that resting capability with the resolved hits; `FrozenPreparedBatch.positions` and `every_position_is_supported` expose only centers carrying its support proof. `SupportBatchValidator.rejects_iff` and `CheeseCommand.prepare_none_iff` make exact-batch rejection equivalent to at least one frozen lookup miss.

10. Atomic spawn and preservation: `CheeseCommand.miss_refuses_atomically` returns the exact input world on any support miss. `SpatialSpawn.freshKey_injective` and `freshKey_not_old` prove fresh copy allocation; `CheeseCommand.success_adds_exactly_fifty_one`, `success_preserves_fixed`, `success_preserves_fixed_field`, `success_preserves_wellFormed`, and `successful_repeated_ticks_preserve_wellFormed` prove exact population growth, fixed-field preservation, one-step closure, and arbitrary successful repeated-tick closure.

Guarded negative witnesses: `Ano.SpatialNegativeWitness` checks that foreign equal-shaped habitats cannot combine pointwise, foreign frames cannot substitute for `World3`, a player-plane vector cannot enter a world-vector slot, a physical row is not a cell reference, a layout is not sealed lineage, a `Mars3` point cannot enter a `World3` locator, and placement is not localization. ## Third spatial-contract milestone map

1. Affine torsors and additive maps: `AffineFrame` separates points from displacement vectors and checks the zero, associativity, subtraction, and cancellation laws. `AdditiveVectorMap` and `AffineMap` prove identity, composition, and extensionality laws; `AffineEquiv` adds identity, symmetry, composition, and explicit inverse round trips on points and vectors.
2. Scalar-linear registered embeddings: `SemiringLaw`, `ModuleLaw`, `LinearVectorMap`, `LinearAffineMap`, `AnchoredLinearEmbedding`, and `RegisteredLinearAffineEmbedding` add scalar compatibility without pretending that every affine map is linear, invertible, dimension-preserving, or isometric. `RegisteredLinearAffineEmbedding.place_smul` ties the exact registry `OffsetMapToken` to the checked linear action.
3. Weighted interpolation: `WeightedSupport` requires finite nonempty duplicate-free normalized support, while `WeightedSupport.Nonnegative` remains an independent optional convexity certificate. `WeightedSpan` turns each support fiber into source and destination lineage; its fiber enumeration, membership, uniqueness, normalization, constant-reproduction, segmented-reduction, and row-permutation laws are checked.
4. Registered interpolation lineage: `SpatialInterpolationRegistry` and private-constructor `RegisteredInterpolationRows` authorize a frozen interpolator for exact frame and habitat endpoints. `Lineage.interpolationSource` and `Lineage.interpolationCell` are the authorized factories retaining both legs, and the registered gather and sample theorems agree with the mathematical span operations.
5. Partial live `Position`: `PartialColumn`, `PositionColumn`, and `SpatialWorld` model entity component presence as `Fin entityCount → Option (Point frame)`. `commitCheese_old_position`, `commitCheese_fresh_position`, and `commitCheese_position_exhaustive` prove that all old rows, including absence, are preserved and all 51 fresh rows receive the exact supported positions.
6. Exact live player anchoring: private-constructor `LiveLineage` and `LivePlayerAnchor` tie a singleton selected live entity, its stored `some anchor`, its resolver token, and its exact pre-world together. `toLinearPattern` retains the exact registered affine embedding, and its seed law proves that every generated seed is the stored player anchor plus the registered local offset action.
7. Accepted exact-51 live spawn: private-constructor `LiveCheeseRequest` fixes the entity carrier to the input population and the spatial pattern to that exact world. Success adds exactly 51 live rows, writes every fresh `Position`, preserves all old positions and fixed fields, returns only supported payloads, and preserves well-formedness; refusal is the exact input state.
8. Repeated dependent ticks: `LiveCheeseResolver` rebuilds a request from each successor world rather than reusing the first world's `Fin` carrier or anchor. `liveCheeseTicks_wellFormed` proves finite successful iteration preserves the schema and well-formedness through changing entity populations.
9. Heterogeneous registered column bundles: `RegisteredInput` and `Bundle` admit any finite family of carrier types only when every fixed or located field, registered weighted interpolation, or certified present live position is aligned onto one shared query domain. `aligned_interpolatedField` is tied to exact `RegisteredInterpolationRows` plus its `WeightedValueLaw`, and `EntityPositionEffect.apply_hit_registered_inputs` proves the resulting n-ary point is exactly what the actual partial ECS `Position` scatter writes. `PositionMapping` remains the typed mathematical evaluator; Steel still owes the checked operation language that may construct it.
10. Certified entity and lattice effects: `EntityPositionEffect` requires injective live destinations and proves hit, miss, fixed-field, population, and well-formedness laws for a real partial `Position` write. `LatticeDestinations` retains exact situated/locator authority, while `LatticeAssignment` proves field-level hit and miss laws without claiming a general schema-valid world mutation.
11. Advanced guarded rejections: `Ano.SpatialAdvancedNegativeWitness` checks that raw interpolation source and cell functions are not sealed lineage, a generic anchored linear embedding is not a registered one, a foreign-frame point cannot enter an interpolator, and a raw function cannot enter a registered fixed-field bundle input.

## Trust and scope

The theorem boundary is a proof-carrying semantic plan. `OrdinaryUpdate` supplies local and global preservation laws plus a conservative footprint frame law; `Schema` supplies its global invariant and spawn-preservation law. The proofs establish that every accepted value at this boundary preserves the same registered schema and that every validation refusal is atomic.

Physical equal cardinality supplies only `Nonempty (Iso A B)`. It does not choose a semantic alignment. The guarded checks in `Ano.NegativeWitness`, `Ano.SpatialNegativeWitness`, and `Ano.SpatialAdvancedNegativeWitness` verify that foreign domains, habitats, frames, layouts, placements, locators, interpolation legs, affine embeddings, and raw bundle maps do not substitute for registered semantic authority.

Registry authorization is proved for sealed `Lineage`, registered location and interpolation rows, live entity/component lineage, registered affine embeddings, exact live cheese requests, and registered heterogeneous bundle inputs. Generic `LocatorRows`, `WeightedSpan`, `PositionMapping`, `Scatter.assign`, `Plan`, and `OrdinaryUpdate` remain mathematical metalanguage. In particular, public plain scatter can express a colliding raw destination and a trivial schema can package an arbitrary field replacement; the theorem boundary applies only to the sealed registered wrappers until Steel constructs and checks the corresponding certificates.

The commutative merge theorem applies only when the supplied operation satisfies the exact associative, commutative, and identity equations. IEEE floating-point addition does not satisfy those equations and therefore cannot instantiate that theorem honestly.

The third milestone proves the abstract affine and weighted-interpolation contract, but it does not yet instantiate concrete 2-D/3-D coordinate modules, units, a metric, orthogonality, isometry, or error-bounded floating-point refinement. `Boxed` still proves only nominal box-coordinate round trips rather than bounded integer-lattice lookup. `SupportSpec.candidate`, its score, and `RestingPose.supported` remain proof-carrying inputs; the kernel does not derive ray, interval, incidence, unit-normal, collider, nearest-distance, or no-penetration laws from engine geometry.

The worked pattern remains an arbitrary injective `Fin 51 → SpatialVector` supplied in the proof object; Lean does not yet prove the analytic sine, cosine, square-root, golden-angle implementation, IEEE refinement, or dense lowering. The companion `SpatialWorld` performs an actual partial ECS `Position` extension for all 51 fresh entities and preserves old presence exactly. This is one certified component family around the core `World`, not yet a general multi-component schema, archetype transaction, persistence model, or Steel implementation.

Steel certificate construction, a restricted evaluator for registered `PositionMapping`, concrete phyllotaxis numerics, engine support/raycast refinement, collision-aware reverse interpolation, promotion of lattice assignment to a schema-valid world action, CBQN or another dense-array refinement, boundary policies, save/load, general partial ECS storage and relationships, despawn repair, and surface-complex theorems remain pending in `foundations.md`.

The source scan contains no `sorry`, `admit`, `unsafe`, or custom `axiom` declaration. The expected-failure witnesses are non-declaring guarded checks, so they add no theorem or recovered declaration to the environment. The governing `#print axioms` audit is axiom-free for the registered linear and lineage bridge laws; list/extensional interpolation and live execution laws use only Lean's standard `propext` and `Quot.sound`, while scatter-backed effects additionally use standard `Classical.choice`. No project axiom enters the kernel.
